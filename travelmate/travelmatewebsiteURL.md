## 📌 Overview

**TravelMate** is a web application designed to simplify how travelers plan, organize, and experience their trips. Powered by WebAssembly and hosted on Wasmer, TravelMate provides fast, responsive, and intuitive tools to make your travel experiences hassle-free.

🔗 **Live Application:** [nahortravelmate.wasmer.app](https://nahortravelmate.wasmer.app/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Smart Itinerary Planner:** Generate customized day-by-day travel plans based on your destination and preferences.
* **Destination Discovery:** Explore popular attractions, local food spots, and hidden gems.
* **Real-time Travel Information:** Get instant access to essential travel tips, weather updates, and local guidelines.
* **Seamless UI/UX:** Fast loading times and responsive design for mobile and desktop users alike.
* **Lightweight & High Performance:** Built and deployed using WebAssembly containerization on Wasmer Edge.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Hosting & Deployment:** [Wasmer](https://wasmer.io/) Edge
* **Runtime:** WebAssembly (Wasm)
* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript / Web Components
